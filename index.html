From the Owner

Furnace Running, No Warm Air.
Fixed Before Morning.

A homeowner called late at night because the furnace was running but the house kept getting colder. We treated it as an emergency and sent the on-call technician right away.

The Diagnosis. After checking the thermostat, safety switches, and gas line, the tech traced it to a worn-out igniter that could no longer light the burners. That single failed part stopped the whole system from heating while the blower kept pushing cold air.

The Fix. The igniter was replaced, the system was tested, and the unit cycled normally. The home was warm again before we packed up. We also answered their questions about furnace and air conditioner replacement costs, so they know what to expect when it’s time to upgrade.
